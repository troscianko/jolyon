---
title: "Penryn mudflats"
excerpt: "The Penryn mudflats offer foraging grounds and refuge for overwintering waders"
themes: [light-pollution]
---

Our work suggests that this light pollution across the mud flats alters the 'fear-of-flight' in endangered curlew, potentially allowing them to see and evade their predators (such as foxes) with higher levels of light pollution. But any alteration of these predator-prey dynamics is likely to have complex and unpredictable consequences.
