---
title: "3D furrows"
excerpt: "Furrows in the gravel bed were angled with or against the light"
theme: [light-pollution]
---

Under direct light, the furrows would either create dark shadows (orthogonal to the light) or be aligned with the light so no shaddows are made. Under diffuse light there are no shadows. This treatment allows us to disentangle the role of light vs dark substrate, from light-vs-dark shadows.
