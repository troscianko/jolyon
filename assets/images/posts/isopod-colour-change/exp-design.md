---
title: "Experimental setup"
excerpt: "Chambers either had direct or diffuse light, 3D grooves, and half-white, half-black backgrounds"
theme: [light-pollution]
---

Sea-slaters were placed in chambers with controlled diffuse or direct lighting, their movemets tracked using infrared video, and colour-changed measured. Each chamber had half white and half black gravel, so the slaters could choose their backgrounds.
