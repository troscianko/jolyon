---
title: "Contrast induction illusions"
excerpt: "These illusions demonstrate that a target's internal pattern contrast is affected by its surrounds"
themes: []
---

Textural contrast induction (left), shows two identical 4x4 targets. The internal contrast of the target on the left appears higher than the one on the right, showing how a low contrast surround can increase the perceived internal contrast of a target.

Orientation contrast induction (right) shows three identical targets that are either rotated or changed to be out-of-phase with the surrounding stripes. These show how perceived internal contrast is affected by stripe orientation and phase, with the lowest perceived contrast when both phase and orientation align with the background.
