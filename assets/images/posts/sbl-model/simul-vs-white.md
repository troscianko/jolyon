---
title: "Simultaneous contrast vs assimilation"
excerpt: "These demonstrate how the perceived lightness of a target is dependent on its background, but a dark background can make something appear either lighter or darker"
themes: []
---

Left: The central bar is a uniform block of grey shown against a gradient, but the bar appears as a gradient that is lighter on the left (against the dark background, doing the opposite of its surround). Right: the grey bars interleaved with the black and white bars are an identical grey value, but they appear darker on the right (where they are interleaved with black bars). So in this case a dark surround makes the target darker, and the target is 'assimilating' the surround’s values. So a dark background can make a target lighter or darker!
