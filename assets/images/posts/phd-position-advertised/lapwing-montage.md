Top-left shows the original (RGB) image, top right shows a bandpass pattern analysis snapshot, used to quantify background pattern matching. Bottom left shows GabRat, a measure of edge disruption designed to measure how much an object's 'true' outline is broken up by patterning. Bottom right shows a colour-clustered image that uses the receptor-noise-limited model to decide whether adjacent colours are discriminable, and joins them if they aren't.
