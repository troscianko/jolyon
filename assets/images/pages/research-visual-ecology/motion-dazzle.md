Left shows an example of a motion illusion - move your head back and forth towards the image and the concentric rings should appear to rotate in opposite directions. Top-right: Zebra stripes are assumed to cause confusion in lions, particularly as a herd is being chased and the stripes all blend together, causing hugely confusing motion cues. Bottom-right: Dazzle marking were used on Allied shipping in WW1 in the hope that they interfered with submarine targetting.
