title: "Cube illusion"
excerpt: "Colour constancy is affected by local appearance"

---

This illusion simulates the one first shown in Purves & Lotto 2003. The left cube is illuminated with a local **simulation** of blue light, and the right with yellow. The squares on the top of the left-hand cube that appear yellow are actually a neutral grey. And the squares on the right-hand cube that appear blue are an identical grey. This is a powerful chromatic illusion that points to an underlying local colour constancy mechanism.

Additionally, the bright white squares that appear to be glowing are actually an identical pixel value to each cube's respective top central 'white' tile. This is a powerful simultaneous contrast demonstration.
