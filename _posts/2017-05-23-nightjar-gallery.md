Here is a selection of my nightjar photos, all taken near Choma in Zambia during fieldwork. Included are images of fiery-necked nightjars *Caprimulgus pectoralis*, Mozambique nightjars (also known as square-tailed nightjars, *Caprimulgus fossii*), and pennant-winged nightjars *Caprimulgus vexillarius.* Almost all of these nightjars are incubating eggs on their nests, which we studied as part of my research into animal [camouflage](http://www.jolyon.co.uk/myresearch/sensory-ecology/camouflage/).
