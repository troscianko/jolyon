Tagging a post `animal-vision` in its front matter is enough to make it appear in the post list
on the [Animal Vision](/animal-vision/) page — including UV photography posts, since that
content lives on the Animal Vision page rather than a separate menu section.

A post can carry several themes at once, e.g.:

```yaml
themes: [animal-vision, visual-ecology]
```

— it will then show up on both the Animal Vision page and the Visual Ecology page.
