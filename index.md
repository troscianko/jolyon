### My research

I have a background in behavioural ecology and sensory ecology, asking questions such as how an animal’s cognition or appearance to other animals affects how they interact with their environment, and how this in turn affects their evolution (see my [publications](http://www.jolyon.co.uk/myresearch/mypublications/)).

I’m currently a NERC Independent Research Fellow, investigating how the light environment and a habitat’s three-dimensional structure affect an animal’s camouflage, see [here](http://www.jolyon.co.uk/2018/01/nerc-fellowship/) for more info.
